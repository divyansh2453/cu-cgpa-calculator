❓ Frequently Asked Questions
How is CGPA calculated in Chandigarh University? ▼
CGPA at CU is the weighted average of all grades since your first semester. Formula: CGPA = Σ(Credits × Grade Points) / Σ Credits. It considers every subject's credit weight and grade point (A+=10, A=9, B+=8, B=7, C+=6, C=5, D=4, F=0).
How to convert CGPA to percentage in CU? ▼
The official Chandigarh University formula is: Percentage = (CGPA − 0.75) × 10. For example, a CGPA of 8.5 = (8.5 − 0.75) × 10 = 77.5%. Some departments use CGPA × 10 or CGPA × 9.5 — always verify with your department.
What is the difference between SGPA and CGPA? ▼
SGPA (Semester Grade Point Average) is calculated for a single semester. CGPA (Cumulative Grade Point Average) is the overall average across all semesters since you joined the university. Your CGPA updates every semester based on all your previous results.
What is the minimum CGPA to pass in Chandigarh University? ▼
For UG programs (B.Tech, BCA, B.Com etc.), the minimum required CGPA for degree eligibility is 4.50. For PG programs (MBA, MCA, M.Tech etc.), the minimum CGPA is 5.0. Students below these thresholds at the end of the first year may lose a year.
How is SGPA calculated in CU? ▼
SGPA = Σ(Grade Points × Credits for each subject) / Total Credits in that semester. For example: if you scored A+ (10) in a 5-credit subject and A (9) in a 4-credit subject → SGPA = (50 + 36) / 9 = 9.56.
Does CU use CGPA or percentage for placements? ▼
Most companies recruiting from CU accept CGPA directly. However, many companies (especially government and PSU) require percentage. Use the official formula: Percentage = (CGPA − 0.75) × 10. Always check the specific company's requirement before applying.
What grade is needed to get 8+ CGPA in CU? ▼
To maintain a CGPA above 8.0, you generally need to score B+ (8) or higher in most subjects. Consistent A (9) and A+ (10) grades across your subjects will push your CGPA towards 9+. Use the Target CGPA Predictor feature on this tool to plan your upcoming semesters.
